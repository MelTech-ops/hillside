Our story

We're more than just a pub

We're your neighborhood gathering spot, where good food meets great company.

The Hillside sits at the corner of North Spring Street in Ansonia, and it has always worked the same way: come down, order something that took real time to cook, and stay long enough to talk to whoever ends up on the stool next to you.

The kitchen smokes its own brisket and pork. The stuffed breads are baked to order, fourteen ways. The taps rotate through local and craft, the cocktails get made with real ingredients, and the specials board changes depending on the day, the season and what looked good that morning.

Sunday through Thursday there's football on every screen. Wednesdays the mic comes out. Thursdays it's steak night. And there's a patio for the months Connecticut gives us one.

What we're known for

  • House-smoked brisket and pork
  • Fourteen kinds of stuffed bread, baked to order
  • Rotating local and craft drafts
  • Game-day specials three nights a week
  • Karaoke Wednesdays
  • Catering, from wing trays to a whole roast pig

Good to know

  • Dine-in and outdoor seating
  • Kitchen open until 10pm daily
  • Bar open late Friday and Saturday
  • Sunday opens at 9am for brunch
  • No online ordering, so call us or come in
